A switch with no moving parts

A transistor is a tap for electricity, and the handle is another voltage. No finger, no lever: one wire decides whether current flows through two others. Every gate in every computer is a few of these. Turn the handle and look inside.

3 · On the bench: one transistor, one light

A 2N7000 transistor, an LED with a 330 Ω resistor, a push-button, and a 100 kΩ resistor from the gate to 0 V. Press the button: the gate goes to +5 V and the light comes on. Your finger is on the handle, not in the current path.

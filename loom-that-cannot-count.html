The loom that cannot count

Jacquard’s loom read a chain of punched cards. A hole lifted a thread; no hole left it down. One card, one row of cloth. Punch the cards, weave the cloth, and find out what a machine can do with instructions but without a single decision.

How the loom reads a card

Each hole lets a hook through, which lifts one lengthwise thread (pink). The shuttle then carries the crosswise thread (blue) under the lifted ones and over the rest.

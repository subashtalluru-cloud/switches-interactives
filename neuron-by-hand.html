A neuron by hand

“Learning” sounds like magic. It is not. A neuron is a line that tries to separate two kinds of dots, and learning is nudging that line whenever it gets one wrong. Turn the knobs yourself first, then let the machine do it and watch it find the same line by pure arithmetic.

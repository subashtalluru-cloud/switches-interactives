What is a character, really?

Type anything, in any script. Below it, the same text at three depths: the characters you see, the numbers Unicode gives them, and the bytes that are actually stored. They do not line up one to one, and every garbled email you have ever received comes from that gap.

The mojibake machine

Your text is sent as UTF-8 bytes, but the receiving program assumes an old Western encoding (Windows-1252), shows the result, and saves it again as UTF-8. Then someone forwards it.
